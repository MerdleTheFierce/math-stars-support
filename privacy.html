Math Stars Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Math Stars is a multiplication practice game for children and families. We designed it to collect nothing.

Information we collect

None. Math Stars does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party services. It works fully offline.

Information stored on your device

When a player creates a profile, the name they type, the avatar they pick, and their game progress (levels, scores, streaks) are saved only on that device. This information never leaves the device, and we cannot see it. Deleting a profile in the app, or deleting the app, removes it.

Children's privacy

Because Math Stars collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, it is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
