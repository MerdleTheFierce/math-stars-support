Common questions

There's no sound. Make sure the in-game button at the top right says Sound ON and your iPhone volume is up. If sound stops after switching apps, tap anywhere in the game, or turn Sound OFF and back ON.

How do I add another player? Tap the player name at the top left, then Add Player. Each player keeps their own scores and levels.

How do I delete a player? Tap the player name at the top left, then the ✕ on that player's card.

Will my child's progress be saved? Yes, on that device. Progress isn't uploaded anywhere, so deleting the app also deletes the saved progress.
